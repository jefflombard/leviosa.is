This website uses a small number of cookies to work properly and to understand how it is used. This notice explains which ones and how to control them.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your choices and see which pages are useful.

## Cookies we use

**Essential cookies**
These keep the site secure and make features such as online booking work. They are always on.

**Analytics cookies**
These show us which pages are visited and how people find us. The data is grouped and never used to identify you. They are only set if you allow them.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may stop online booking from working.
