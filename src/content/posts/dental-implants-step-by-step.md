An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post. Once it has healed, a crown is fitted on top. The result looks, feels and works like a natural tooth.

## 1. Consultation and scan

We start with an examination and a 3D scan to check the bone and plan the exact position of the implant. You receive a written plan with every step and cost.

## 2. Placing the implant

The implant is placed under local anaesthetic in a single appointment of about an hour. Most people describe it as easier than an extraction.

## 3. Healing

Over the next three to four months, the bone grows around the implant and holds it firmly in place. You can wear a temporary tooth during this time if the gap is visible.

## 4. The final crown

Once healed, we take a scan and fit a custom crown matched to the shade of your other teeth.

## Looking after it

Brush and clean around an implant just like a natural tooth, and keep up your hygiene visits. With good care, implants can last for decades.
