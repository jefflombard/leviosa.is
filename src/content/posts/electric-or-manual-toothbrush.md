It is one of the questions we hear most in the hygiene room. The honest answer is that a good technique beats any brush.

## The case for electric

Electric brushes do some of the work for you. Most have a two-minute timer and a pressure sensor, which helps if you tend to rush or brush too hard. Studies show they remove slightly more plaque for most people.

## The case for manual

A manual brush is cheap, light and works anywhere. Used well, twice a day for two full minutes, it cleans just as effectively. Choose soft bristles and a small head that reaches the back teeth.

## What actually matters

- Brush twice a day for two minutes
- Angle the bristles towards the gum line
- Replace the brush or head every three months
- Clean between your teeth once a day

If you are not sure your technique is working, ask at your next hygiene visit. We are happy to show you on your own teeth.
