Most people grow up hearing that a check-up every six months is the rule. It is a good starting point, but it is not the same for everyone.

## Why the interval changes

Some mouths stay healthy for a year between visits. Others need a closer eye. At your check-up we look at your gums, your fillings, your history and how quickly plaque builds up, then suggest a recall that fits you.

- **Every 3 to 4 months** if you have gum disease or a lot of restorations
- **Every 6 months** for most adults and children
- **Every 12 months** if your teeth and gums have been stable for several years

## What happens at a check-up

A routine check-up takes around 30 minutes. We examine each tooth, check your gums and soft tissue, and take X-rays only when they are needed. You leave with a clear note of anything we found and what, if anything, needs doing.

## The cost of waiting

Small problems are quick and cheap to fix. A tiny cavity found early is a simple filling. Left for two years, it can become a root canal or an extraction. Regular visits are the easiest way to keep treatment small.
