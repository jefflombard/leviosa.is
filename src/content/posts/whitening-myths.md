Whitening is one of the most requested treatments at Scarlet, and one of the most misunderstood. These are the myths we hear most often.

## 1. Charcoal toothpaste whitens teeth

Charcoal can remove some surface stains, but it is abrasive and can wear enamel over time. Thinner enamel makes teeth look more yellow, not less.

## 2. Lemon and baking soda are a natural fix

Lemon is acidic and softens enamel. Mixed with baking soda, it scrubs that softened surface away. It is best kept out of your mouth routine.

## 3. Whitening damages your teeth

Professional whitening, planned by a dentist, is safe for healthy teeth. Some people feel short-lived sensitivity, which we manage with gentler gels and shorter wear times.

## 4. Results last forever

Whitening lasts from one to three years, depending on what you eat and drink. A top-up every so often keeps the shade you like.

## 5. Every tooth will change colour

Fillings, crowns and veneers do not whiten. If you have visible restorations, we plan around them so your smile stays even.

## What works

Custom trays made from a scan of your teeth, with a gel strength chosen for you, give the most even and predictable result. Ask for a whitening consultation and we will show you the shade you can expect.
