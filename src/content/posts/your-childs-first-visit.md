A good first visit sets the tone for years of relaxed check-ups. It is less about treatment and more about getting to know us.

## When to book

Bring your child in when their first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Early visits are short and mostly about looking, counting teeth and answering your questions.

## What happens

- A quick look at teeth and gums, often on your lap
- Advice on brushing, bottles and snacks
- A ride up and down in the chair, if they want one

## How to prepare

Talk about the visit as something ordinary. Read a book about the dentist, play at counting teeth at home and avoid words like hurt or scary. Book a time when your child is usually rested, such as mid-morning.

## After the visit

We will suggest when to come back, usually every six months. Bring siblings along when you can. Seeing someone else in the chair is the best way to learn there is nothing to worry about.
