## The role

You will see your own list of patients across check-ups, fillings, crowns and root canals, with support from Marco on implant cases. First visits are booked at forty-five minutes, so there is always time to talk.

## What you bring

- A dental degree and full registration
- At least two years in general practice
- A calm manner with nervous patients
- An interest in explaining treatment clearly, without pressure

## What we offer

- A guaranteed base salary, not a percentage of what you sell
- Paid training days and a yearly course budget of €2,500
- Your own nurse and a modern surgery with a 3D scanner
- Free care plan for you and your family
