## The role

You will run hygiene visits, deep cleans and gum care for adults and children. Every appointment is forty-five minutes, so you can do the job properly and still have time to coach patients at home care.

## What you bring

- A hygiene qualification and full registration
- A gentle, unhurried approach
- Confidence explaining gum health in plain words

## What we offer

- Fixed hourly pay, with paid breaks
- Ultrasonic and air-polishing equipment in a dedicated room
- A yearly course budget of €1,200
- Free care plan for you and your family
