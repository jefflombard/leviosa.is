## The role

You will assist in treatment, prepare and sterilise instruments and look after patients before, during and after their visit. You will work mostly with one dentist, so you get to know their patients too.

## What you bring

- A dental nursing qualification
- At least one year in practice
- Care for detail, especially around infection control

## What we offer

- Regular hours, finishing by 18:30
- Paid training towards radiography or implant nursing
- Free care plan for you and your family
