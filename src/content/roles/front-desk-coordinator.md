## The role

You will answer calls and messages, book appointments, welcome patients and explain written plans and prices. On busy mornings, you will be the one who keeps the day running smoothly.

## What you bring

- Experience in a customer-facing role, ideally in healthcare
- A warm, patient phone manner
- Comfort with booking software and simple payments

## What we offer

- A set weekly schedule, planned a month ahead
- Full training on our booking system
- Free care plan for you and your family
