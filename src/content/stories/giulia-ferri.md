## Where it started

Giulia wanted a brighter smile for her wedding and assumed veneers were the only way. Her teeth were healthy and well shaped, just stained from years of coffee.

## The plan

Erik suggested starting with take-home whitening instead. It would keep her natural enamel, cost far less and leave veneers as an option if she ever wanted them. She took the written plan home and came back a week later.

## The result

After three weeks of wearing custom trays for an hour a day, her teeth were six shades lighter. She keeps a tube of top-up gel for before special occasions.
