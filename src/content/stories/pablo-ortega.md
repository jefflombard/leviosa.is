## Where it started

Pablo mentioned on the booking form that he was nervous and had not been to a dentist in almost twenty years. We gave him a sixty-minute first appointment instead of thirty, so nothing would feel rushed.

## The plan

Elena spent the first twenty minutes just talking. The exam found two small cavities and some tartar, nothing that needed anything drastic. Pablo left with a written plan: two fillings and a hygiene visit, with every price listed.

## The result

Over six weeks, the fillings and clean were done in short visits, each one with a pause signal agreed in advance. He has since booked his six-month check-up himself.
