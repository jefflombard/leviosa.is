Elena has worked in general dentistry for more than fifteen years. She sees most new patients herself, so the first visit starts with a conversation rather than a chair.

## What she looks after

- Check-ups and treatment plans for adults and children
- Fillings, crowns and root canal treatment
- Patients who feel nervous about the dentist

## Outside the clinic

She spends weekends on the coast with her two children and still takes her own check-ups every six months.
