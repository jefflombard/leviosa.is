A check-up is the simplest way to keep treatment small. We look for problems early, when they are quick and inexpensive to fix.

## What is included

The exam, any X-rays you need, a mouth cancer screening and your written plan are all part of the price. There is no extra charge for questions, and follow-up calls about your results are free.

## How often

Most adults and children come every six months. If your teeth and gums have been stable for years, we may suggest once a year. If you have gum disease, every three to four months.

## Before your visit

Bring a list of any medications you take and let us know if anything has changed since your last visit. If you feel nervous, tell the front desk and we will book extra time.
