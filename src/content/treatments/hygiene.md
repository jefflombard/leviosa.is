Even the best brushing misses a little. A hygiene visit removes the build-up you cannot reach at home and keeps your gums healthy.

## Who it is for

Everyone benefits from a regular clean, but it matters most if your gums bleed when you brush, you have braces or implants, you smoke, or you are pregnant. We will suggest how often to come based on your gums, not a fixed schedule.

## Does it hurt?

Most people find a clean comfortable. If your gums are sensitive, we can use a numbing gel and work more slowly. You can raise a hand to pause at any time.

## Afterwards

Your teeth may feel smoother and a little sensitive for a day. Brush as usual that evening. We will suggest when to come back, usually every six months.
