An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small titanium post. Once healed, a custom crown is fitted on top. It looks, feels and works like a natural tooth.

## What the price covers

The price includes your 3D scan, the implant, the healing cap, a temporary tooth and the final crown. If you need a bone graft first, we will tell you at the consultation and add it to your written plan before you decide.

## Is it right for me?

Implants suit most adults who have lost one or more teeth. We check your bone, gums and general health first, and explain the alternatives, such as bridges and dentures.

## Looking after it

Brush and clean around an implant just like a natural tooth and keep up your hygiene visits. With good care, implants can last for decades.
