Professional whitening lifts years of staining safely, with a result that still looks like your own smile.

## Take-home or in-clinic

Most patients choose take-home trays: they give an even, natural result and cost less. If you need a brighter smile quickly, for a wedding or an event, we can add a one-hour in-clinic session to speed things up.

## Is it safe?

Whitening planned by a dentist is safe for healthy teeth and gums. Some people feel short-lived sensitivity, which we manage with a gentler gel or shorter wear times.

## How long it lasts

Results last one to three years, depending on what you eat and drink. Keep your trays and top up with a little gel when you feel your shade has dropped.

Fillings, crowns and veneers do not change colour, so we plan around any visible restorations.
